## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, gallery-grade digital identity for high-end contemporary body art, piercing, and precision laser removal in Guayaquil. It deliberately departs from dated tattoo subculture tropes—avoiding unreadable blackletter typography, exaggerated gothic motifs, and distressed grunge textures—in favor of a disciplined, clinical yet artistic high-contrast visual environment.

The aesthetic fuses contemporary fine-art gallery curation with technical dermatology standards: deep carbon backgrounds, crisp graphite divisions, generous spatial cadence, and architectural display typography. The tone communicates absolute hygienic rigor, master craftsmanship, and bespoke luxury. High-intent conversion paths are engineered around seamless direct consultations via WhatsApp, treating booking inquiries with concierge-level clarity.

## Colors

The palette relies on deep carbon darkness, mineral graphite surfaces, and stark ivory typography to frame portfolio works with gallery-level neutrality, while electric blue and communication green direct user action.

- **Foundational Surfaces:** 
  - Canvas Base: `#0D0D11` (Deep Carbon).
  - Raised Surface: `#121218` (Obsidian Base).
  - Surface Container: `#1A1A24` (Graphite Layer).
  - Elevated Container / Borders: `#222230` (Structural Line).
- **Typography & Content:**
  - High-Emphasis Content: `#FAF9F6` (Off-white / Fine Ivory).
  - Body & Secondary Copy: `#D8D6D0` (Muted Bone).
  - Structural Subtext & Captions: `#888694` (Graphite Ash).
- **Accents & Conversion:**
  - Technical Accent / Primary Action: `#2563EB` (Electric Cobalt), supported by `#3B82F6` for hover interactions and `#00D2FF` for micro-highlights and specialized service tiers (e.g., laser precision).
  - High-Priority Conversion: `#25D366` (WhatsApp Emerald), reserved exclusively for direct concierge chat triggers, consultation CTAs, and active availability badges.

## Typography

The typographical architecture juxtaposes the raw, industrial power of Oswald with the neutral precision of Inter. 

- **Headlines (Oswald):** Styled exclusively in uppercase or strong title casing with subtle letter-spacing expansions on display tiers. This creates a vertical, poster-like impact reminiscent of contemporary art manifestos and luxury atelier portfolios.
- **Body & Structural Information (Inter):** Strictly utilitarian. Body copy remains unstylized and legible against low-luminance surfaces, adhering to robust contrast ratios.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Captions and technical metadata (such as tattoo needle gauges, session durations, healing timeline markers, and laser wavelengths) alternate between Inter semi-bold and Oswald spaced-caps to signify rigorous procedural documentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous boundary margins that replicate the negative space of a modern art catalog. 

- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column grid, 48px outer canvas margins, 24px column gutters. Section blocks use expansive vertical margins (`margin` token scaled via layout compositions) to allow visual art and before-and-after laser documentation sufficient presence.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, 32px canvas margins, 20px gutters. Multi-column portfolio showcases gracefully fold into dynamic 2-column or asymmetrical editorial stacks.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column grid, 20px outer margin, 16px gutters. Structural hierarchy prioritizes vertical thumb flow, high-contrast imagery, and persistent, non-intrusive floating contact anchors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft drop shadows or artificial light simulations. Instead, the design system utilizes dark tonal layering and hairline structural borders to communicate depth:

- **Surface Layering:** 
  - Base level (`#0D0D11`) holds the global viewport canvas.
  - Card tiers and module trays use `#121218` or `#1A1A24` to separate content panes naturally.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** All containers, image frames, and interactive fields are delineated by a crisp 1px border (`#222230`). On active, focus, or hover states, borders transition cleanly to `#2563EB` or an ivory boundary `#FAF9F6/20`.
- **Frosted Elements:** Fixed floating bars (such as the sticky WhatsApp inquiry anchor and mobile navigation headers) utilize backdrops with a 12px blur over an 85% alpha `#0D0D11` fill to maintain context without masking underlying artwork.

## Shapes

The design system implements a soft, architectural corner geometry (Level 1). Interactive controls, cards, input panels, and image frames strictly utilize subtle radius treatments (4px standard, 8px on select elevated containers) to deliver a disciplined, modern look. 

Buttons and badges avoid pill shapes to prevent a casual or playful aesthetic, maintaining the precise feel of a high-end medical-grade design atelier. The only circular elements permitted are thumbnail artist avatars and status dot indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Technical Blue):** Solid `#2563EB` background with `#FAF9F6` text. 4px corner radius. Strict padding (`space-sm` vertical, `space-lg` horizontal). Hover transitions to `#3B82F6` with subtle inner hairline clarity.
- **Direct WhatsApp Conversion:** Distinctly styled with `#25D366` solid background and `#0D0D11` high-contrast typography, flanked by the official micro-icon. Reserved exclusively for direct artist consultations, quote estimation flows, and laser assessment requests.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background enclosed by 1px `#222230` border with `#FAF9F6` text. Transitions to `#1A1A24` background and `#FAF9F6` border on hover.

### Cards & Gallery Modules
- **Portfolio Art Card:** Flat `#121218` background, 1px border `#222230`, 4px corner radius. Image ratio kept strict (4:5 vertical portrait for body art, 1:1 for detailed piercings). Hover applies an ultra-subtle border illumination in `#2563EB`. Typography inside features artist name, technique categorization, and an instant "Cotizar este estilo" WhatsApp micro-link.
- **Before / After Laser Showcase:** Dual-pane comparative container with a vertical slider or synchronized side-by-side display, framed in `#1A1A24` with session count and wavelength badges rendered in `label-caps`.

### Chips & Filter Badges
- Filter selectors for styles (e.g., *Blackwork, Microrealismo, Piercing Titanium, Cover-up, Laser Nd:YAG*) use `#1A1A24` background, 1px `#222230` outline, and `label-md` type. Selected state transitions to `#2563EB` background with `#FAF9F6` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#121218`, border 1px `#222230`, text `#FAF9F6`, placeholder `#888694`. Focus state replaces border with `#2563EB` accompanied by an imperceptible 1px glow. Field labels sit above in `label-md` uppercase with muted bone styling (`#D8D6D0`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 18px boxes with 2px radius and 1px border `#222230`. Active state fills with `#2563EB` housing a sharp white checkmark. Radio buttons follow identical logic with nested 8px solid center marks.

### WhatsApp Concierge Float
- A persistent, docked corner action module utilizing `#25D366` surface treatment with dynamic availability text ("Cotizaciones directas — Marcelo Tattoo GYE") that persists seamlessly across desktop and mobile browsing.
